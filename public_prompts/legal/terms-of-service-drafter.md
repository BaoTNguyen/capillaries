Draft Terms of Service for **{{product_name}}**, a **{{product_type}}** operated by **{{company_name}}**.

**Service details:**
- Pricing model: {{pricing_model}}
- User types: {{user_types}}
- Key features: {{key_features}}

Include the following sections:
1. **Acceptance of Terms** — how agreement is formed (click-wrap, browsing, etc.)
2. **Service Description** — what is provided and what is explicitly not guaranteed
3. **User Accounts** — registration, authentication, account security responsibilities
4. **Acceptable Use** — permitted and prohibited activities, with specific examples
5. **Intellectual Property** — ownership of content (user-generated vs. platform), license grants in both directions
6. **Payment & Billing** — pricing, billing cycles, refund policy, price change notification
7. **Termination** — grounds for suspension or termination, effect on user data
8. **Disclaimers** — warranty disclaimers, "as-is" language
9. **Limitation of Liability** — liability caps, exclusion of consequential damages
10. **Dispute Resolution** — governing law, arbitration vs. litigation, class action waiver
11. **Modifications** — how terms may be updated, what constitutes continued acceptance

Write in plain language. Bold key obligations. Output as a structured document with numbered sections.
