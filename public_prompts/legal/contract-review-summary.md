Review the following contract and produce a structured summary for non-legal stakeholders.

**Contract text:**
{{contract_text}}

**Your summary must include:**
1. **Parties & Effective Date** — who is bound and when it starts
2. **Scope of Agreement** — what each party is obligated to do, in plain language
3. **Key Financial Terms** — pricing, payment schedule, penalties for late payment
4. **Term & Renewal** — duration, auto-renewal clauses, notice periods for termination
5. **Liability & Indemnification** — caps on liability, who bears risk for what
6. **Termination Triggers** — conditions under which either party can exit early
7. **Red Flags** — any unusual clauses, one-sided terms, or ambiguous language that should be negotiated

Format each section as a short paragraph. End with a "Recommended Actions" bullet list of 3-5 items for the business team before signing.

Tone: Clear, direct, non-legal language. Assume the reader has no legal training.
