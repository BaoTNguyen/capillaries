Create a brand voice and tone guide for **{{company_name}}**, a **{{company_description}}** serving **{{target_audience}}**.

Core brand values: {{brand_values}}
Current brand personality (if any): {{current_personality}}
Aspirational brands we admire: {{aspirational_brands}}

The guide should include:
1. **Voice Attributes** — Define 4-5 adjectives that describe the brand voice (e.g., "Confident but not arrogant"). For each, provide a "We are / We are not" pair.
2. **Tone Spectrum** — How the voice adapts across contexts: marketing copy, customer support, social media, internal communications, and error/apology messages. Provide a scale from casual to formal for each.
3. **Vocabulary Guide** — Words and phrases we use vs. words and phrases we avoid. Provide 15+ examples in a two-column table.
4. **Grammar & Style Rules** — Contractions, Oxford comma, capitalization, emoji usage, exclamation points, sentence length preferences.
5. **Channel-Specific Guidelines** — Specific dos and don'ts for: website, email, social media, and product UI copy.
6. **Before & After Examples** — Take 5 common messages (welcome email, error message, promotion, support reply, social post) and show a "before" (generic) and "after" (on-brand) version.

Output as a polished style guide document that a new copywriter could use on day one.
