Write a product launch email sequence for **{{product_name}}** by **{{company_name}}**, launching on **{{launch_date}}**.

Target audience: {{target_audience}}
Key value proposition: {{value_proposition}}
Price point: {{price}}
Launch offer: {{launch_offer}}

Create a 4-email sequence:
1. **Teaser Email** (send 7 days before launch) — Build curiosity without revealing everything. Subject line options (3 variations).
2. **Launch Day Email** — Announce the product, highlight top 3 benefits, include a strong CTA. Subject line options (3 variations).
3. **Social Proof Email** (send 3 days after launch) — Feature early testimonials, use cases, or results. Subject line options (3 variations).
4. **Last Chance Email** (send 7 days after launch) — Urgency-driven, remind of the launch offer deadline. Subject line options (3 variations).

For each email provide:
- Subject line (3 A/B test options)
- Preview text
- Full body copy (150-250 words)
- Primary CTA button text
- P.S. line

Tone: {{brand_tone}}. Output as a complete email sequence ready to load into an email marketing platform.
