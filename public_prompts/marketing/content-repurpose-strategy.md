Take the following long-form content and create a repurposing plan that turns it into 10+ pieces of content across multiple formats and platforms.

Original content type: {{content_type}} (e.g., blog post, webinar recording, podcast episode, whitepaper)
Original content title: "{{content_title}}"
Original content summary: {{content_summary}}
Key points covered: {{key_points}}
Target audience: {{target_audience}}

For each repurposed piece, provide:
- **Format** (e.g., LinkedIn post, tweet thread, infographic, short video script, email newsletter, carousel, quote graphic, blog snippet)
- **Platform** where it will be published
- **Angle** — the specific take or slice of the original content
- **Draft Copy** — A complete, ready-to-use draft (not just a description)
- **Estimated Production Time** (quick = <30 min, medium = 1-2 hours, heavy = half day+)

Prioritize high-impact, low-effort pieces first.
Include a distribution timeline suggesting which pieces to publish on which days over a 2-week period.
Output the plan as a numbered list, followed by a calendar table.
