Build a detailed customer persona for **{{company_name}}**'s **{{product_name}}** product.

What we know about our customers:
- Industry: {{customer_industry}}
- Company size: {{company_size_range}}
- Typical buyer role: {{buyer_role}}
- Common pain points: {{pain_points}}
- How they currently solve the problem: {{current_solutions}}

Create a comprehensive persona with:
1. **Persona Name & Photo Description** — A realistic, memorable name and a brief visual description
2. **Demographics** — Age range, job title, education, income bracket, location type
3. **Professional Context** — Company type, team size they manage, reporting structure, KPIs they own
4. **Goals** (3-4) — What success looks like in their role
5. **Frustrations** (3-4) — Daily pain points and blockers
6. **Buying Behavior** — How they discover tools, who influences their decisions, typical buying timeline, budget authority
7. **Information Sources** — Publications, podcasts, communities, events they frequent
8. **Day in the Life** — A brief narrative (5-6 sentences) describing a typical workday
9. **How We Win Them** — Key messages, proof points, and channels that resonate
10. **Objections & Responses** — Top 3 objections they would raise, with counter-arguments

Output as a single-page persona profile that a sales or marketing team can pin to their wall.
