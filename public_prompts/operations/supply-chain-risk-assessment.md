Conduct a comprehensive supply chain risk assessment for **{{company_name}}** in the **{{industry}}** industry.

Supply chain overview:
- Key products/services: {{key_products}}
- Critical suppliers: {{critical_suppliers}}
- Geographic regions of supply: {{supply_regions}}
- Tier 1 supplier count: {{tier1_count}}
- Single-source dependencies: {{single_source_items}}
- Annual procurement spend: ${{procurement_spend}}
- Current inventory strategy: {{inventory_strategy}}

Recent disruptions or near-misses: {{recent_disruptions}}

Perform the following assessment:
1. **Risk Identification** — Categorize risks across these dimensions:
   - **Supplier risk** — Financial health, capacity constraints, quality issues, single-source dependency
   - **Geopolitical risk** — Trade policies, sanctions, political instability in supply regions
   - **Logistics risk** — Transportation bottlenecks, port congestion, carrier reliability
   - **Natural disaster risk** — Climate events, pandemics, seismic activity in key regions
   - **Demand risk** — Forecast accuracy, demand volatility, bullwhip effect
   - **Cyber/technology risk** — Supplier IT vulnerabilities, data integration failures
2. **Risk Scoring Matrix** — For each identified risk: Likelihood (1-5) x Impact (1-5) = Risk Score. Sort by score descending.
3. **Critical Path Analysis** — Identify the single points of failure that would halt operations if disrupted.
4. **Mitigation Strategies** — For each high-priority risk: specific preventive actions, contingency plans, and estimated cost to mitigate.
5. **Supplier Diversification Plan** — Which single-source items need alternates? Recommended qualification timeline.
6. **Business Continuity Recommendations** — Safety stock levels, dual-sourcing strategy, nearshoring opportunities.
7. **Monitoring Framework** — Early warning indicators and how frequently to review each risk category.

Output as an executive risk report with a heat map description and prioritized action plan.
