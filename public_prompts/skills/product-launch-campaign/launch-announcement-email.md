# Launch Announcement Email

You are an experienced product marketing copywriter. Draft a launch announcement email for the product described below. The email should generate excitement, clearly communicate value, and drive a specific action.

## Product Details

- **Product name:** {{product_name}}
- **What it does:** {{product_description}}
- **Key benefits (top 3):** {{key_benefits}}
- **Target audience for this email:** {{email_audience}}
- **Launch date:** {{launch_date}}
- **Call to action:** {{cta}} (e.g., sign up for early access, book a demo, start free trial)
- **CTA link:** {{cta_url}}
- **Tone:** {{tone}} (e.g., professional-excited, understated-confident, bold-disruptive)
- **Special offer or incentive (if any):** {{launch_offer}}

## Writing Instructions

1. **Subject Line Options:** Provide 3 subject line variations:
   - One curiosity-driven (creates intrigue without clickbait)
   - One benefit-driven (leads with the primary value)
   - One social-proof-driven (references early adopters, waitlist size, or partner validation)

2. **Preview Text:** Write a 40-60 character preview text that complements each subject line.

3. **Email Body Structure:**
   - **Opening hook (2-3 sentences):** Start with a pain point or aspiration that resonates with {{email_audience}}. Do not start with "We are excited to announce."
   - **The reveal (1-2 sentences):** Introduce {{product_name}} as the solution.
   - **Three benefit blocks:** For each of the top 3 benefits, write a short heading and 2-3 sentences. Focus on outcomes, not features.
   - **Social proof element:** Include a placeholder for a customer quote, beta user stat, or credibility marker.
   - **CTA section:** Clear, single call to action with urgency framing if appropriate.
   - **P.S. line:** A conversational closing line that reinforces the core message or adds a secondary CTA.

4. **Constraints:**
   - Total email body: 200-300 words
   - Reading level: Grade 8 or below
   - No jargon unless the audience expects it
   - Mobile-friendly: short paragraphs, scannable structure

## Output Format

Return all three subject line + preview text combinations, then the full email body. Mark placeholders with [brackets] where the sender should customize.
