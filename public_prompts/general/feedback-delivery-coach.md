Help me prepare to deliver feedback in the following situation:

**Feedback recipient:** {{recipient_name_or_role}}
**Relationship:** {{relationship}} (e.g., direct report, peer, skip-level, manager)
**Situation:** {{situation_description}}
**Specific behavior observed:** {{behavior}}
**Impact of the behavior:** {{impact}}
**Desired outcome:** {{desired_change}}

Generate:

1. **Opening statement** — a warm, direct opener that signals this is a constructive conversation, not an ambush
2. **SBI framework delivery:**
   - **Situation:** When/where the behavior occurred (specific, factual)
   - **Behavior:** What you observed (observable actions, not interpretations)
   - **Impact:** How it affected you, the team, or the work (concrete consequences)
3. **Forward-looking request** — what you'd like to see instead, framed as a positive ask rather than a criticism
4. **Anticipated pushback** — 2-3 likely defensive responses and how to address each calmly
5. **Closing** — how to end on a collaborative note, including a follow-up plan

Also provide:
- 2 things to **avoid saying** (with explanation of why)
- A suggested **follow-up check-in** timeframe and format

Tone: Empathetic, specific, and growth-oriented. This should feel like coaching, not a performance review.
