2. **Provide context** — what happened, why, and what it means for the audience specifically
3. **Address likely concerns** — anticipate 2-3 questions this audience will have and answer them preemptively
4. **State next steps** — concrete actions being taken, with timelines

- No corporate jargon ("synergy", "leverage", "alignment")
