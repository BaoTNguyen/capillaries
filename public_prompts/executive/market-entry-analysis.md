Conduct a market entry analysis for **{{company_name}}** considering expansion into **{{target_market}}**.

**Current business:**
- Core product/service: {{core_offering}}
- Current markets: {{current_markets}}
- Annual revenue: {{revenue}}
- Competitive advantage: {{competitive_advantage}}

Analyze the following dimensions:

1. **Market Sizing** — TAM, SAM, SOM for the target market with methodology and assumptions. Use both top-down and bottom-up estimates.
2. **Competitive Landscape** — identify 5-8 existing players, their positioning, market share estimates, strengths and weaknesses. Map on a 2x2 (price vs. differentiation).
3. **Regulatory Environment** — licenses, certifications, legal barriers, compliance costs, timeline to market readiness
4. **Customer Analysis** — target segments, buying behavior, switching costs, willingness to pay, channel preferences
5. **Entry Strategy Options** — evaluate at least 3 approaches (organic build, acquisition, partnership/JV, licensing) with pros, cons, capital requirements, and time-to-revenue
6. **Financial Projections** — 3-year P&L estimate for the recommended approach, including investment required, breakeven timeline, and ROI
7. **Risk Factors** — political, economic, cultural, technological, and competitive risks with severity ratings
8. **Go/No-Go Recommendation** — clear recommendation with supporting logic and conditions that would change the answer

Output as a structured report suitable for a leadership team strategy review. Include an executive summary at the top (one page max).
