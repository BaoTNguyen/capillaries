Create a strategic initiative brief for the following proposal:

**Initiative name:** {{initiative_name}}
**Sponsor:** {{executive_sponsor}}
**Problem statement:** {{problem_statement}}
**Proposed investment:** {{budget_range}}
**Target timeline:** {{timeline}}

The brief must include:

1. **Strategic Context** — how this initiative connects to company-level OKRs or strategic pillars. Why now, and what happens if we don't act.
2. **Opportunity Sizing** — TAM/SAM/SOM if market-facing, or cost savings / efficiency gains if internal. Include assumptions and sensitivity ranges.
3. **Proposed Approach** — high-level solution design, phasing (crawl/walk/run), and key milestones
4. **Resource Requirements** — headcount, technology, budget by quarter. Distinguish new spend vs. reallocated spend.
5. **Risk Analysis** — top 5 risks with likelihood, impact, and mitigation plan
6. **Success Metrics** — 3-5 measurable KPIs with baseline, target, and measurement method
7. **Decision Framework** — what criteria should leadership use to evaluate this? Include kill criteria (conditions under which we should stop).
8. **Alternatives Considered** — at least 2 alternatives with pros/cons vs. the proposed approach

Format as an executive-ready document with clear section headers. Include a one-paragraph "elevator pitch" at the top that captures the initiative in 50 words or less.
